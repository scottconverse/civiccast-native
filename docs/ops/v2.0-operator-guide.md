# CivicCast v2.0 Operator Guide

Audience: live operators, playback operators, and bulletin-board operators running day-to-day station workflows.

## Daily Opening Checks

1. Confirm Safe to broadcast in System Health.
2. Verify backup status is ready.
3. Confirm source readiness for live meetings, uploaded VOD, CG bulletin board, and remote ingest.
4. Confirm captions are either local, external-appliance reviewed, or intentionally disabled for the event.
5. Check schedule/now-next and EPG export windows before going live.

## Core Workflows

- Public meeting or event: schedule the event, confirm source, start live session, monitor captions, finalize recording, review transcript, publish, archive.
- VOD playback: confirm playback policy, gated/open status, preroll metadata, catalog visibility, and archive status.
- CG bulletin board: select board, inspect zones, preview render/snapshot, publish only after preview.
- Contributor media: review submissions, approve or reject, and keep contributor status messages clear.
- Remote ingest: preview ingest plan and confirm credentials before treating relay output as proven.
- Facility routing and overlays: preview router take commands, scheduled takes, L-bar/squeezeback plans, and overlay z-order before live use.

## Do Not Do

- Do not publish unreviewed external captions.
- Do not send router commands to hardware unless the station has approved the route and attached equipment.
- Do not describe local preview as live hardware proof.
- Do not bypass backup or restore rehearsal after an update.

## Evidence To Keep

Keep screenshots or exported reports for Safe to broadcast, schedule/EPG, caption review, publish/archive result, analytics report, and any external dependency proof.
