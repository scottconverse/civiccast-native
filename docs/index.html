Planning for government web accessibility?

DOJ’s current Title II guidance lists April 26, 2027 for governments serving 50,000 or more people, and April 26, 2028 for smaller governments and special districts. The requirements cover more than captions and include exceptions. Captioning tools support your accessibility work; they do not establish compliance on their own.

BEHIND THE CREATOR

Scott Converse.
Technology for the public good.

Scott is the co-founder of Longmont Public Media, Longmont, Colorado’s public-access television station, and serves on its Board of Directors. His experience connects the software behind a station with the community it serves.

His four-decade career spans software development, product creation, and high-tech startups, including executive roles at Apple, Motorola, Paramount Pictures/Viacom, and MCI. He founded startups including ClickCaster, Medioh, and ExtractCraft.

Alongside that technology career, Scott has co-founded organizations built for the public good: Longmont Public Media, the Longmont Observer, and TinkerMill. CivicCast brings those two strands together—software development and community media—in an independent, open-source project.

Your station should not depend on one person.

The Apache-2.0 license lets you use, inspect, and adapt the source. Operator instructions, recovery guidance, architecture, and API documentation give your team or an IT partner a place to start if the original maintainer is unavailable.

Who helps if playout stops at 7 p.m.?

Your station needs a local operator or IT contact for immediate help. CivicCast offers documentation and community support through GitHub—not a staffed emergency line or a guaranteed response time.
