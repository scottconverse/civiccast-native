# Procurement And Legal Posture Brief

Status: current beta adoption guidance

This brief is plain-English project information for early adopters. It is not legal advice,
procurement advice, or a certification of compliance. Stations
should have their own counsel, records officer, accessibility lead, and IT
review any production deployment.

## Project Posture

CivicCast is open-source civic broadcast software for public meetings,
community media, public-access operations, nonprofits, schools, and similar
organizations that need to stream, preserve, and publish public-interest video.

The project is designed to reduce dependence on proprietary broadcast
appliances by giving stations inspectable software, local control, exportable
records, and a clearer proof trail.

## Data Ownership And Export

CivicCast is intended to run under the station's control. Meeting recordings,
captions, summaries, signed records, support bundles, and archives belong to the
station or organization operating the deployment, subject to that station's
policies and applicable law.

The software should not require a proprietary hosted account to export station
records. Operators should preserve local backups and archive exports before any
major update or migration.

## Public Records And Retention

CivicCast can help create, review, package, sign, publish, and archive meeting
records. CivicCast does not decide what is a legal record for a jurisdiction and
does not certify a retention schedule.

Use CivicCast retention fields and export logs as operational aids. A records
officer or counsel should decide retention policy, disclosure rules, and whether
auto-generated captions or summaries can be published before human review.

## Accessibility Posture

CivicCast includes accessibility-oriented workflows such as captions, translated
captions, public portal accessibility checks, and operator copy that separates
ready, warning, and blocked states.

This is an accessibility posture, not a legal compliance certification. A
station should run its own accessibility review for public deployment,
especially for branded portals, embedded players, kiosk displays, and any
jurisdiction-specific requirements.

## AI Captions And Summaries

CivicCast uses local AI workflows for captions, translation, and sourced
summaries. Auto-generated captions and summaries can be wrong. Records clerks
should review names, numbers, votes, motions, dates, and any claim that affects
meaning before publication.

Sourced summaries are aids for review and discovery. They are not legal minutes
or a substitute for station approval.

## Incumbent Platform-Style Comparison

CivicCast can be evaluated as an open-source alternative for some incumbent-like
software workflows: meeting capture, public portal publication, archive exports,
proof logs, channel-style scheduling, and feed-based connected-TV reach.

Do not claim full industry-standard parity for hardware output, proprietary automation,
SDI/DeckLink (professional video hardware interfaces), physical headend delivery
(facility cable-system transmission), support contracts, or platform app
certification unless a station has separately proven those paths.

The fair early-adopter comparison is cost, ownership, transparency, export, and
vendor lock-in posture for software-owned workflows.

## Procurement Language

Potential procurement description:

> CivicCast is open-source civic broadcast software for public meetings and
> community media. It provides a self-hostable workflow for meeting broadcast,
> publication, captions, reviewed summaries, signed record exports, archive
> copies, subscriber notifications, and software-defined channel/connected-TV
> (CTV) proof. The station retains operational control of its data and may
> inspect, modify, and support the software under the Apache-2.0 license.
