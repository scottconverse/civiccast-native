# v1.0 Readiness Proof - Retention Presets + Pilot Deferral

Date: 2026-05-15

Scope: remediation for v1.0 readiness findings DEC-001 and FIELD-001.

## Decisions

FIELD-001 is deliberately moved after the 1.0 milestone. v1.0 must not claim a
completed seven-day real-hardware pilot. The post-1.0 pilot remains required
before public adoption claims, certified-integrator claims, or "non-Scott
production deployment" claims.

DEC-001 is closed for v1.0 by shipping a top-10-state retention preset subset.
The subset is operator-facing guidance, not legal advice. Each preset carries a
source URL and a review note so the operator or records officer can confirm the
local schedule before enabling automatic purge behavior.

## v1.0 Retention Subset

The v1.0 subset covers these states:

| State | Minimum days | Trigger |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| CA | 30 | 30 days after the local-agency recording is made |
| TX | 90 | 90 days after approval of official minutes |
| FL | 730 | 2 anniversary years after adoption of official minutes |
| NY | 1825 | 5 years for qualifying posted open-meeting video recordings |
| PA | 365 | 1 year after approval of official minutes |
| IL | 540 | v1.0 review floor pending agency schedule confirmation |
| OH | 540 | v1.0 review floor pending approved RC-2 schedule |
| GA | 90 | 90 days after minutes are prepared and verified |
| NC | 30 | after official minutes are approved unless recording is official minutes |
| MI | 365 | 1 year minimum for required public-meeting recordings |
