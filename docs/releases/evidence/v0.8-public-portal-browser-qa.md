## States Covered

- Success: live section, coming-up section, published recordings, and follow
  controls render together.
- Empty: live, schedule, and recordings explain what residents should do next.
- Partial: a failed schedule section does not hide available recordings.
- Loading: delayed asset data announces loading before resolving.
- Error recovery: invalid subscription token preserves the API's actionable
  recovery copy.
- Confirmed: signed confirmation link reports that notices will be sent.
- Already confirmed: repeated confirmation link stays harmless and actionable.
- Unsubscribed: signed unsubscribe link reports that notices have stopped.
- Mobile: channel RSS and podcast RSS links remain visible at 375 px width.

## Accessibility And Console

- axe-core WCAG 2.0 A/AA, 2.1 A/AA, and 2.2 AA serious/critical gate passed.
- Keyboard interaction checked by pressing Enter on subscription and caption
  controls.
- Browser console check on the main resident view reported zero console errors.
