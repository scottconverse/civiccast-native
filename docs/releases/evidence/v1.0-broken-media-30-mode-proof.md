## Assertions

For every mode, the suite asserts:

- the mode ID is stable and unique;
- the input is sanitized and generated locally in a temporary directory;
- `pack_vod_asset` fails as `PackagingError`, not an unhandled exception;
- the error carries the expected rendition and diagnostic context;
- `pack_slate_fallback` succeeds after the failure;
- the fallback manifest is valid HLS and points to the slate variant;
- the slate playlist exists and has `#EXT-X-ENDLIST`.
