## Facts (U22, OBSERVED)
- Live caption ASR: faster-whisper `medium`, CUDA float16, beam 1, 3 channel callers on one shared model; each
  batch is one 10 s window (5 s segment + 5 s overlap); station throughput 1.86x the audio cadence - the MEAN keeps
  up (per-channel mean 2.8-3.4 s vs a 5 s cadence).
- The TAIL does not: single windows of 10.6-19.5 s occur (reproduced off-live), often hitting 2-3 channels within
  milliseconds (a shared GPU stall). The tap's persistence gate (U11: 15 scans = 30 s over `max_backlog_segments`=2)
  then trips and the channel is PAUSED under exponential backoff: 120 s, then 240 s, up to 480 s observed
  (19 overloads in 67.5 min live). A ~16 s stall therefore costs 2-8 minutes of no captions, and the acceptance
  verifier samples those minutes as "no embedded cues" -> FAIL.
- The overlap is the stabilizer's confirmation mechanism: dropping it drops words (U22 section 4). Keep 5 s.

## Decision (coordinator, engineering)
Replace "pause the channel" with "catch up": when a channel's settled backlog exceeds the limit, keep captioning
from the NEWEST audio and discard only the oldest settled segments that can no longer be captioned in time, so the
channel never stops captioning for longer than the backlog it sheds. No exponential pause for a GPU-bound stall.
