## Why
Whole-program two-pass lands programs near -16, but meetings and talk programmes have long quiet stretches (a soft
speaker, a far microphone), so a 240 s window can sit at -18.3 even when the whole program reads -16.4. The LRA knob
cannot fix that (U20 section 12). Broadcasters fix it with a speech leveler/AGC ahead of the loudness normalizer.
Also, AAC encoding adds about +1.2 to +1.3 dB of true-peak overshoot on dense material (U20 section 14), so the
normalizer's TP target needs headroom.

## Decision (coordinator, engineering)
Put a leveling stage BEFORE loudnorm in the preparation audio chain, using filters built into the shipped FFmpeg
(LGPL; no new dependency). Keep two-pass loudnorm for the integrated target. Lower the loudnorm TP target to give
AAC headroom. Measure first, then implement the gentlest chain that passes.
