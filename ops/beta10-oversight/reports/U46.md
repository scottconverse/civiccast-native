## 2. Root cause, and the path it blocks

**OBSERVED (root cause, proven by measurement in §3.2):** the caption sidecar's program clock is an
**absolute, unbounded second count** — `chunk_index * segment_seconds`, rendered by
`webvtt.format_webvtt_timestamp` through `divmod(total_ms, 3_600_000)`, so it has no 24-hour wrap and
keeps growing for the life of the station. The three timed-text parsers typed the hours field as
`\d{1,2}`, so on the first window written after 100 hours **every** window stopped matching and the
live parser returned **0 cues** from a file full of them. Public crossed 100 h; government and
education have not.
