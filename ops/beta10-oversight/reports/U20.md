## 18. Doubts

1. **The band criterion may be unsatisfiable by any preparation-side setting**, not just by LRA. Two of the
   four sources are outside −16 ± 1 on some 240 s window at *every* LRA tested, by up to 2.1 LU. The
   acceptance rung measures the emitted audio, so if the band is a hard acceptance requirement, the
   question is not which LRA but whether the material's dynamic range can be conformed at all without a
   dynamic-gain approach this filter does not implement here.
2. **`normalization_type=dynamic` may be a build-specific behaviour** rather than a general ffmpeg fact. I
   only have this build (`C:\Program Files\CivicCast (Native)\dependencies\ffmpeg\bin\ffmpeg.exe`). If a
   future build honours `linear=true`, the whole LRA analysis would have to be re-run — the setting could
   then matter.
3. **Deleting `test_two_pass_probe_failure_fails_closed`** is the one judgement call in this part that a
   reviewer might reasonably reverse (§10.2). It is a pure test edit, in its own commit, trivially revertible.
4. **EF4 at LRA=7 passes with true peak exactly `-1.0` dBTP** — the ceiling is `<= -1.0`, so it passes by
   0.0 margin. That is one rounding step from failing, and I would not treat EF4 as robust evidence that
   LRA=7 is safe even if the band criterion were relaxed to admit it.
5. **The seek-free arbitration rests on three windows of two files.** It is decisive for those windows
   (the IS/EX disagreement is structural, not marginal) but it is not a survey; if a future unit relies on
   window-level I/LRA across many files, the arbitration should be widened.
