## 3. Why this is a decision and not something I should just fix

Keeping U30's guarantee and keeping U21's rebind are jointly impossible **in the current shape**: U21
rebinds on every genuine new worker session, and a rebind creates a new relay incarnation. Any fix
changes one of two audit-established behaviours:

- Carrying the latch across the rebind (making the heal latch survive a rebind) would mean a channel
  whose relay healed once and then genuinely lost its worker could never heal again in that episode —
  i.e. it would freeze until a human restarts it. That is the exact failure mode U30 was written to
  remove.
- Letting the latch stay per-incarnation (what the merged code does) means the storm guard's "closed"
  invariant becomes "bounded by the two budgets".

Both are defensible; neither is mine to pick. Also note U30's own passing test
(`test_u30_the_escalation_leaves_the_relay_child_alone`, renamed by `9cce9012` to
`..._does_not_stop_the_relay_child_itself`) still asserts `fixture.relay.stopped == []`,
i.e. U30's *decision* not to stop the relay holds — the failure is in the end-to-end consequence, not
in U30's own code path.

## 4. Options, with the one I would pick

**A — Accept the merged behaviour; narrow the older guard test. (recommended)**
The guard test's intent ("no restart storm") is preserved by two budgets that now exist and are tested:
U30's ≤3 worker restarts per rolling hour (`_FREEZE_ESCALATION_RESTART_BUDGET` /
`_FREEZE_ESCALATION_BUDGET_WINDOW_S`) and the relay heal's one-shot latch per incarnation. Rewrite
`test_daemon_tick_sequence_no_restart_storm_with_frozen_playlist` to assert the *bounded* property (one
heal per relay incarnation; total relay children bounded by the escalation budget; no growth after the
episode's last restart), and record the weakening in the hls_relay finding-4 note. Cost: one test
rewrite + a doc note. Risk: an audit's negative test is relaxed by the very unit it constrains — needs
the owner's assent, which is why this is a question.

**B — Carry the heal latch across an escalation-caused rebind only.**
At `hls_relay.py:1110` (`self._drop_channel_relays(channel_id)`, inside the `if new_session:` branch
opened at `:1102`) capture `heal_attempted` / `heal_baseline_segment` for
keys whose teardown is caused by the escalation, and re-apply them in `_ensure_relay`'s fresh record;
leave a genuinely new worker session re-arming as today. Cost: new supervisor state + tests, and a new
daemon→supervisor seam (`apply` would need to learn *why* the session changed). Risk: a cured-but-still-
frozen channel has no further relay move — it falls back to U30's worker restarts, which is arguably
correct but is a new ladder.

**C — Replace the one-shot latch with a rolling relay-heal budget** (symmetric with U30's worker
budget), so re-arming after a rebind is structurally bounded rather than latched. Cleanest long-term
shape; largest change, touches finding-4's whole rationale, and should get its own unit and audit round.
