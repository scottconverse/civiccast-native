## 4. The reachable analogue — and the product question it raises

There **is** a reachable shape in which the boundary provider answers the same closing item with a
small remainder: the recorded horizon falls *short* of the item's real media end. That is the
sign-flipped twin of the archived incidents (there the recorded end was ~11 s **late**; here it is
early by the leg's own startup latency, because `_establish_horizon_from_dispatch` dates a
non-deferred plan from the dispatch instant `now` while the leg starts airing a moment later).
Probing at that horizon returns the same item with its remaining seconds, non-empty, so the code
dispatches a normal deferred rollover onto that stub leg. The engine then reaches EOS on it, and
the clean-exit watchdog relaunches onto the next item — the 2.437 s / 6.61 s dead-air shape in
`reports\U26.md` §1.1, but with no exit 1.

**The product question: what should the station do with a live item's short remainder at the
horizon?**

- **(a) Today:** roll onto the remainder as its own (stub) leg; the next item is picked up by the
  EOS clean-exit relaunch, which costs the measured dead air.
- **(b) Append the next scheduled item after the remainder in the same plan** (extend the closing
  plan past its slot boundary by ≤30 s), so the rollover arms the next item and the switch is a
  deferred in-worker switch — no EOS, no relaunch, no dead air, and **no content is cut**. This is
  the honest reading of "roll seamlessly onto THAT item" for this shape.
- **(c) What the decision literally says for this shape** — treat the remainder as ≤ floor and
  start the next item at `plan_end_at`, early by the remainder. Under a *deferred* switch that is
  the same instant as (b) (the switch happens at the outgoing leg's end either way), so (c) buys
  nothing over (b) and describes a cut only if the switch is immediate.

**My recommendation:** (i) **withdraw the branch change as written** — §3 shows it is dead code;
(ii) if the reachable shape is worth fixing, the form to build is **(b)**, not a tail cut: it
removes the EOS relaunch dead air without unauthorised content loss, and it does not break D42's
"no following item starts while the current one still has media"; (iii) keep `21d9a715` +
`e6ddb89e`/`21e9ee17` as the delivered fix for brief items 1-2. If you have a live log line that
shows `target=filler` (or an arithmetic step in §3 you read differently), send it and I will
implement against that shape instead.
