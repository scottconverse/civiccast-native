## The questions

1. **Is the committed fix still wanted?** Commit `b0f2e49c` ("cap a plan segment by the media's real
   playable length") makes `_segment_duration` cap the row-derived duration by the media's real
   measured length, and adds the U36 test suite (`773234bb`, `14da353f`, `61d8c6d7`). On this
   station's measured data — 0 of 539 rows over, worst 0.994 s *short*, and the incident's own
   `lpmrot-14` row 0.10 s short — **the cap never bites**: it is a class-level guard for a
   disagreement that does not exist here. It is inert for the observed incident. Do you want it
   kept as a guard (it is harmless, tested, and would catch a station whose rows *do* overstate),
   or should it be reverted and the real fix aimed at the relaunch path?

2. **Is the relaunch-path bookkeeping the thing to fix instead?** The observed sliver is the
   relaunch re-resolving a plan for the program whose media has already fully aired, using
   `elapsed_seconds` derived from the schedule clock. Two candidate rules, both product decisions
   and neither implemented:
   - (a) on relaunch, treat an item as exhausted when the *media* has played out (not when the
     clock says so), and hand off to the next program; or
   - (b) make the schedule clock and the leg's actual start agree (the extended-schedule rotation's
     start times), so the arithmetic cannot drift in the first place.
   I recommend (a) as the smaller, local change; (b) is the deeper correctness fix but touches the
   rotation builder.

3. **Does the 30 s schedule tail floor belong on the relaunch path?** `daemon._avoid_schedule_tail_plan`
   (`daemon.py:4333-4386`, `_SCHEDULE_TAIL_FLOOR_SECONDS = 30.0`) is deliberately skipped under
   `if not force_fallback and boundary_at is None:` — so it does not apply to horizon-bound
   rollovers, and it is never applied on the relaunch path either. A 30 s floor there would have
   turned the 2 s sliver into "start the next program early". Product decision; not implemented.

## 5. The question for the owner — I am not picking

The bound, the parallelism, and the budget are policy:

- **(a)** Give the warm its **own** bound — derived from the asset's media duration or a rate budget
  — distinct from the interactive `CIVICCAST_EGRESS_PREPARATION_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` (300 s), which exists
  to bound a *resident-visible* preparation.
- **(b)** Let the warm use `_foreground_thread_cap()` instead of `-threads 1` — the `-threads 1`
  choice at `:2486` exists to be polite to the live channel, so this trades encode throughput for
  on-air headroom.
- **(c)** Declare the current behavior correct and budget it: a relaunch costs ~90–145 s and a longer
  resident-visible outage; no code changes.
- **(d)** Decide whether a multi-GB whole-asset entry for a 4.4 h asset should be cached at all at
  18.8/20 GB, and whether `CIVICCAST_CONFORM_CACHE_GB` should move.
- **(e)** Should an asset that cannot be conformed inside the warm bound be **permanently excluded**
  (a negative-cache marker), so the station stops paying a guaranteed-to-fail 300 s warm on every
  airing?

**Related and material to the same decision:** item 1's correct behavior makes `trimmed` **always
true on a relaunch**, so for any asset longer than the warm can handle, resuming from the schedule
