## 1. Two premises in the brief are `ffprobe stream.duration` accounting, not defects

The brief's cause hypothesis named "the 30.000 vs 29.76 s mismatch" and "why audio output is
1.83 s per 2 s segment". **OBSERVED**: both are AAC-in-MPEG-TS `stream.duration` accounting —
it counts whole AAC frames (`N × 1024 / 48000`) and drops the tail frames. Neither is the audio
on the wire, and the station watchdog does not read it (it reads first-packet PTS).

- Slate file: `duration=29.760000` (1395 × 1024/48000) against audio packets that actually span
  `1.400000 .. 31.394667` = **29.994667 s**; video `duration=30.000000` spans 30.000 s. Real
  spans agree to 33 ms, so there is no 240 ms mismatch to explain.
- HLS segment `seg000000100.ts`: `duration=1.834667` (86 × 1024/48000) against 94 audio packets
  spanning `202.587978 .. 204.571978` = **1.984 s**, versus 60 video packets over 1.967 s.

Full output: `evidence\u27-slate-desync-offlive\ffprobe-duration-artifact.txt` and
`slate-source-facts.txt`. **INFERRED**: had I trusted the brief's frame here, I would have gone
looking for a muxer rate bug that does not exist; the desync is real but it is a *branch
hand-off* desync, not a duration mismatch.

## 2. The reproduction needs the live caption/sink shape, not the sub-chain count alone

**OBSERVED** (2×2 in `reports\U27.md` §1): 12 sub-chains with live captions + live sinks
desynced (+19.997 s step, 1717 discontinuities); 12 sub-chains *without* them did not, over 87
segments; 1 sub-chain with them did not, over 28. The defect also fired only once in ~4
boundaries on the reproducing shape. **INFERRED**: the extra live branches change the timing at
the hand-off. I could not pin it further, and I say so in the report rather than claiming "every
sub-chain boundary desyncs".

## 3. Product question — the multi-sub-chain hand-off itself is still there for non-slate legs

The fix removed the multi-sub-chain slate **plan** (one long stream-copied fill, one sub-chain),
which is the right scope for this bug and also retires the 12-sub-chain teardown U13 wedged on.
But `civiccast/egress/gst/engine.py:949-994` still builds one decoder sub-chain per plan segment
with **two independent concat aggregators** (`vconcat_*` and `aconcat_*`) for every other leg —
a real program with N plan segments still hands off N times with nothing coupling the video seam
to the audio seam.

**The question for the owner:** should the engine be hardened so a sub-chain hand-off cannot
advance one aggregator without the other (e.g. a single concat with both pads, a
`streams-selected`-style coupling, or a seam-time gate), or is the slate fix accepted as
sufficient because real program legs are bounded by `plan_segment` sizes that do not hit this?

**My recommendation if I had to pick:** leave it for now and log it — I have **no evidence** that
a real program leg desyncs (mode b/d controls at 1 sub-chain were clean and I never ran a
multi-segment *program* leg through the caption shape), and the mechanism is inferred, not
instrumented. But it is a latent risk on a path residents hear, so it belongs in the backlog
rather than in a footnote. I did not decide it, and I did not touch `engine.py`.
