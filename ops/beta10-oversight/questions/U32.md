## Q2 (product question — needs the owner; this is why I stopped)

**The station is chronically at or over caption capacity, and U23 answered that by discarding
audio instead of blanking captions. Is that the intended production behaviour, and does the
operator/audience learn that it happened?**

OBSERVED, same station, two windows:

| | pre-install (8 h 17 m) | post-install (34 m) |
|---|---|---|
| `is behind` deferrals | 404 | 93 |
| fail-closed PAUSE (captions blanked) | 89 → **2 496 channel-seconds blanked** | 0 |
| catch-up SHED (audio discarded, captions continue) | 0 | 11 → 46 segments, **230.0 s of audio** |

Both windows are over capacity. The difference is the *response*: old = stop captioning for a
while; new = drop the oldest audio and keep captioning. The coordinator's "captions shed every
few minutes" is the new response firing ~ every 2.2 min. So this is not a regression — it is U23
doing what it was built to do, on a pipeline that has been under-provisioned for many hours.

Three decisions I should not make for you:

1. **Is "drop the oldest audio silently" acceptable for a public-record meeting?** The three-tier
   publish non-negotiable (§2.6) is about the *recording*, which is unaffected — but the *live
   captions* are now, by design, allowed to have holes. For a municipal meeting where a resident
   is speaking, a silent 5–15 s caption gap is a materially different product from a visible
   "captions paused". Do we owe the viewer a visible degraded state (a lower-third "captions
   temporarily unavailable", or a caption overlay note) instead of a silent gap?
2. **What is the intended capacity envelope?** Currently `medium` / float16 / `num_workers=3`
   against three channels of 5 s segments with 5 s of ASR overlap — i.e. ~2 batches per channel
   per 5 s, 6 bpm demanded per channel, ~18 bpm total, against an observed ~32 bpm ceiling *on
   this box with coder units running*. That headroom is thin and disappears the moment the ASR
   median rises (observed p95 7.97 s, max 18.02 s under load). Options, none of which are mine to
   pick: cap concurrent captioning to two channels and let the third be explicitly uncaptioned;
   drop to `small` on this hardware; give captions a dedicated GPU; or accept and document the
   shed cadence as the station's operating envelope.
3. **What is the shed budget supposed to be, and is the operator told when it is exceeded?**
   `DEFAULT_CATCH_UP_SHED_LIMIT = 3` inside `DEFAULT_CATCH_UP_SHED_WINDOW_SECONDS = 300.0` only
   escalates to the fail-closed PAUSE ladder. With 11 sheds in 34 min, the station is running at
   roughly 5 sheds / 300 s — *above* the limit — yet 0 pauses were recorded post-install. INFERRED:
   the shed window is evaluated per channel, so 11 station-wide sheds spread across three channels
   stay under 3-per-channel-per-300 s. **If the intent was a station-wide budget, that is a bug;
   if it was per-channel, the station can silently discard audio indefinitely.** Please confirm
   which is intended — this is the one thing here that could be a real code finding, and I have
   not treated it as one because I cannot tell intent from the code.

**Note for whoever picks this up:** `DEFAULT_CATCH_UP_SHED_LIMIT = 0` disables catch-up shedding
and restores the pre-U28 blank-the-captions behaviour — an operator escape hatch already present,
if you would rather have visible pauses than silent gaps while the capacity question is decided.
