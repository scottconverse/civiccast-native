## Q1 (blocking the only speed lever) — what is the minimum acceptable live tap overlap?

**Why it blocks:** the tap overlap is the *only* configuration lever that moves
batch latency, and it is simultaneously the *only* mechanism that confirms a
caption word. `stabilize.py:124` refuses the same window twice
(`if window <= self._last_word_window: return []`), so a word reaches air only if
a **later, advancing window** re-observes it — i.e. only because of the overlap.
`tap_worker.py:514` already forbids overlap 0 outright.

**Measured, real settled segments, 12 windows per arm, stabilizer's own counters
(`%TEMP%\u22\probe.py`, `conf.py`):**

| tap overlap | window | on-air cues | on-air words | expired unconfirmed |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.0 s | 5 s | **0** | **0** | 12 |
| 1.0 s | 6 s | 3 | 7 | — |
| 2.5 s | 7.5 s | 10 | **51** | — |
| 3.5 s | 8.5 s | 11 | 71 | — |
| **5.0 s (shipped)** | 10 s | 10 | **87** | 10 |

And the latency it buys (3 channels concurrent, paired, n=90/arm):

| overlap | mean | p95 | max | round-wall p95 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5.0 s | 2203 ms | 2590 ms | **10 568 ms** | **5.426 s** |
| 2.5 s | 1645 ms | 2200 ms | 2343 ms | 2.269 s |

Overlap 2.5 s buys **−15 % p95** and cuts on-air words by **41 %**. Overlap 1.0 s
buys more and cuts them by **92 %**. This is a **product judgment** — how much
caption text a station may lose to gain ~0.4 s of p95 — and I will not pick it.

**Question:** is 2.5 s acceptable? Is any overlap below 5.0 s acceptable? Or is
"captions pause occasionally" the preferred failure mode over "captions are
thinner but on time"?

## Q2 (drift finding, needs recording somewhere) — `station-set.json`'s caption block is stale and actively misleading

`C:\Program Files\CivicCast (Native)\station-set.json` declares:

```json
"caption_compute_type": "int8",
"caption_device": "cpu",
"caption_model_root": "components/captions-large-v3/..."
```

**None of this is in force, and the named model is not installed.** The station
supervisor computes the device itself (`native/station_runtime.py:961`
`resolve_whisper_device`) and injects it into the control-plane child's
environment (`:1434`); the runtime reads exactly those env names first
(`captions/runtime.py:668`, `:670`), and `build_caption_runtime` passes no
device/compute_type at all, so the env is the *only* source. The box actually
runs faster-whisper **medium** from `packs\captions-floor\models\`, CUDA,
float16.

**Question:** should this be filed as a drift finding against the release, or is
the `runtime` block in `station-set.json` understood internally to be vestigial?
It cost this unit real time to disprove, and it will cost the next engineer the
same.

## Q3 (product question) — is it intended that *every* live cue is low-confidence and ~half the words never air?

Not what U22 was asked, but it is in the measured data at every overlap tested:
**100 % of committed cues carried `low_confidence=True`** against
`low_confidence_threshold = 0.75` (`stabilize.py:40`) — confidences on 10 s
windows ran 0.13–0.75 — and about **half the transcribed words expired
unconfirmed** (`_review_words`, `stabilize.py:251`) and never reached air.

**Question:** if the live station is continuously emitting low-confidence cues
while discarding half the words, the ~3 s latency may not be the first caption
defect. Should a follow-up unit look at the stabilizer's confirmation geometry
before anyone tunes the model?

## Q4 (brief premise correction) — the batch is 10 s of audio, not ~5.4 s

The brief states "~900 segments/600 s → ~2.7 segments (~5.4 s audio)/batch". The
station's own 600 s phase summary reports
`asr_process_batch count=335` with per-channel counts of 117 / 115 / 103 — i.e.
roughly **one window per settled segment**, and with the shipped 5 s overlap
each window is **10 s of audio**. So the station decodes ~3350 s of audio per
600 s of wall.

This does not change the headline (ASR does take ~3 s/batch) and every figure in
`reports/U22.md` is computed from the observed numbers rather than the brief's,
but the brief's per-batch audio figure is off by ~2× and the segment count by
~2.7×, so it should not be carried into the next unit.
