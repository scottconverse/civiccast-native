### Why the rung only caught this once in fifteen

The checker's continuity test compares each segment's **first video PTS** with
the previous segment's **first video PTS + its own video duration** — a
*boundary* test. When the muxer's segment cut falls *inside* the video-less
interval, the segment's `stream.duration` spans the gap and the boundary test
passes; the 14 passing changeovers are in that state. When the cut falls *at*
the interval, the previous segment's video content is short and the next
segment's first frame is far away, and the boundary test fires — education
loudness-03 (0.700 s). **The checker has no intra-segment gap test, so it
under-detects this by design.** Proposed (not written — see "Not started"):
add an intra-segment step test, `max(pts[i+1]-pts[i]) > PTS_FRAME_TOLERANCE_TICKS`,
which would have caught all three.

## The question (what the station SHOULD do)

When the outgoing picture has run out (`outgoing EOS observed stream=video`)
and the incoming leg's first video buffer has not yet arrived, what should air
for that sub-second interval? The candidates and their on-air consequences:

* **A. Gate the commit on the incoming leg's first video buffer** (extend the
  drain gate to the new leg). Cheapest, and it uses machinery that already
  exists. But the outgoing leg's video has already EOS'd, so this adds no
  picture — it may only delay the commit and lengthen the audio tail.
* **B. Pre-roll the incoming leg** (lookahead; the "chain two plans" family that
  U53 item 1 answered). The only candidate that airs real picture at the
  boundary. It is a scheduler change with a wider blast radius.
* **C. Hold / duplicate the outgoing leg's last frame across the handoff.**
  Closes the gap visually, but writes a synthetic frame into the public-record
  archive - that touches the archival non-negotiable (§4.6) and needs an owner
  call, not a coder call.
* **D. Change nothing in the station; record a narrated reload boundary as an
  expected event in the rung's checker** (e.g. annotate rather than FAIL the

  must not undo the coordinator's recent tightening (`CAPTION_QUIET` needing a

  also the only thing that catches a real collapse.
